Why does a schedule say data is unavailable?

Playrush does not invent games, scores or plays. If a sports provider is unavailable or does not supply a requested historical feed, the site shows an honest unavailable state instead.

When does a wager settle?

Wagers stay open until the Game Center receives an official final result. A winning wager credits its displayed payout; a losing wager settles without a payout. A game that is already final cannot accept a new wager.

Can I wager on a future game?

Yes, when that game is available to the connected sports provider and its wagering window is open. Future games use the pregame multiplier shown in the Game Center.

Can I cash out Rush Coins?

No. Rush Coins are virtual Playrush currency and are not redeemable for cash.

What happens if my Store payment succeeds?

Stripe sends Playrush a verified payment event. The server fulfills the order and credits the purchased Coins or entitlement. If the browser closes during checkout, the verified payment record can still be fulfilled.
